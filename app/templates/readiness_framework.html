SCORING LOGIC

How the range is calculated · 準備度範圍怎麼算

Available100% of criterion weight
Partial50% of criterion weight
Missing0% of criterion weight
Not requiredExcluded from denominator
Awaiting0% in minimum; full weight in maximum

Minimum readiness = confirmed earned weight ÷ all required weight. Maximum readiness assumes every still-unknown criterion later proves available. Once all required criteria are reviewed, the range collapses to one final score.
